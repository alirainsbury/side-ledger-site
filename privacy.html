Privacy policy

It collects nothing.

Effective 14 October 2026

Side Ledger is built to keep your business records yours. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no server of its own. Your data lives on your devices and in your own iCloud. The one optional connection it can make, to YNAB, is read-only and runs directly between your device and YNAB.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Side Ledger app for Mac, iPhone and iPad, published by A Rainsbury ("the developer", "I"). It applies to everything the app does, including the optional YNAB link and in-app purchases.

Information collected by the developer

None. The app does not collect, transmit or store any personal information with the developer or with any service the developer runs. There is no sign-in, no user profile, no analytics or crash reporting, and no advertising or tracking identifiers. I cannot see your clients, gigs, invoices, expenses, mileage, receipts or bank transactions.

Where your records live

  • On your devices. Everything you enter is stored in the app's own database on each device.
  • In your iCloud. The app syncs that database between your Mac, iPhone and iPad through Apple's CloudKit, in the private database of your own Apple Account. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud. The developer has no access to it.
  • Receipt photos you attach are stored alongside the expense they belong to, on your devices and in your iCloud, and nowhere else.
  • Files you export (invoice PDFs, CSV and XLSX reports, and the full data export) go only where you save or share them.

The YNAB connection (optional)

If you choose to connect YNAB, Side Ledger uses YNAB's official API to read transactions from the plan and accounts you select, so it can suggest which invoice a payment belongs to. In detail:

  • What is read: incoming transactions in the accounts you choose (date, amount, payee name, memo, account and cleared status), plus the names of your plans and accounts so you can pick them.
  • What is written: nothing. The app has read-only access and never adds, edits, categorises, approves or deletes anything in YNAB.
  • Where it goes: directly from YNAB's servers to your device. No request passes through any server run by the developer.
  • What is kept: the transactions it reads are stored in the app's database on your devices and in your iCloud, like the rest of your records, so suggestions work on every device.
  • Your sign-in: the app signs you in through YNAB's own OAuth page and never sees your YNAB password. The access tokens YNAB issues are kept in your iCloud Keychain, which Apple encrypts end to end. They are shared only between your own devices.
  • Disconnecting: Settings → YNAB → Disconnect deletes the tokens from your Keychain. You can also revoke Side Ledger's access at any time from YNAB's Account Settings, and that takes effect immediately.

YNAB's handling of your data is covered by YNAB's privacy policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app only asks the App Store which Side Ledger products your Apple Account owns. It never sees your name, payment details or purchase history, and the developer receives only the anonymous sales reports Apple provides to every developer.

Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight and the widget

These features run on your device. The Shortcuts actions, Spotlight index and widget show your own records to you and send nothing anywhere.

Network access

The app connects only to Apple (iCloud sync, the App Store, and Apple's font and system services) and, if you connect it, to YNAB (api.ynab.com and app.ynab.com). It makes no other network connections.

Third parties

No data is shared with anyone else. There are no third-party SDKs, analytics services or advertising networks in the app.

Deleting your data

Delete records in the app and they are removed from your devices and your iCloud. Settings → Data → Erase removes everything the app holds. Deleting the app removes its local data; iCloud data can be removed from Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Children

The app is intended for adults running a business. It collects no personal information from anyone, so it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
