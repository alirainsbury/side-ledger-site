Getting started

Three steps from a gig in your calendar to a paid invoice in your report.

Add your suppliers

Each supplier gets its own invoice prefix, rate card and (if they need one) a purchase-order step. Set up your business profile once.

Log gigs, expenses and miles

Add gigs by hand, with Siri, or straight from a calendar event with the included shortcut. Mark a gig attended and the mileage is logged for you.

Invoice and report

Raise an invoice from one or more gigs, export the PDF, email it, mark it paid (or let YNAB spot the payment), then export the tax-year report.

Questions & answers

Where is my data kept? +

On your devices, synced through your own private iCloud database. There are no Side Ledger accounts and no server of ours in between. The developer cannot see your records. See the privacy policy.

Can I get my data out? +

Always. Settings → Data exports everything as a zip of plain JSON plus your receipt images, and you can import it back on another device or keep it as a backup. Reports export as CSV and XLSX. None of that needs a purchase.

What do I have to pay for? +

Two things, both optional. PDF export is a one-time purchase and covers exporting, sharing and emailing invoice PDFs. YNAB Sync is a monthly or yearly subscription. Everything else, including previews, email text, reports and the full data export, is free.

Purchases are handled by the App Store. Use Restore Purchases on a new device, and manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings.

How does the YNAB link work? +

Settings → YNAB signs you in with YNAB (read-only). You pick the plan and the accounts your invoice payments arrive in. Side Ledger then reads new incoming transactions and suggests which invoice each one pays. Nothing is marked paid until you tap Confirm, and Not this one hides a payment for good.

Side Ledger never writes to YNAB. It cannot add, edit, categorise or delete transactions.

Pro forma, PO, invoice: what is the flow? +

For suppliers who need a purchase order, Side Ledger raises a pro forma first. When the PO number arrives, add it and the same document becomes the invoice. Suppliers who do not need a PO go straight to invoice. Invoices can be re-issued at any time.

How does mileage work? +

Give each venue a round-trip distance from home. When you mark a gig attended, a mileage entry is created for you at HMRC's simplified rate. You can change the number of trips (multi-day gigs often need more than one) or add entries by hand.

Which tax year does the report use? +

The UK tax year (6 April to 5 April) by default, on an invoice-date or paid-date basis, with your own custom year-end if you need one. Expense categories map to the boxes on the SA103F self-employment pages.

Siri, Shortcuts and the widget +

Side Ledger adds actions to Shortcuts and Siri: Add Gig, Mark Gig Attended, Get Gigs Needing Invoices, Get Unpaid Invoices, Add Expense, Add Mileage, Open Invoice and Open Gig. They appear after the app's first launch. The widget shows what needs invoicing, what is awaiting a PO and what is unpaid.

What do I need to run it? +

A Mac running macOS 26 or later, or an iPhone or iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later, signed in to iCloud. It is a universal app, so one purchase covers all three.
